# Genre brief — Chill Pop / Indie Electronic / Downtempo

Reference vibe (user-provided): breathy, introspective female vocals, soft
synth pads, a laid-back electronic beat, clean piano/plucked-synth outro,
with subtle Lofi / Ambient Pop elements.

## Best genre search terms
- Chill Pop / Indie Pop
- Downtempo / Chillout
- Ambient Pop / Dream Pop
- Electronic Chill / Chillstep (soft, vocal-driven variety)
- Lofi Chill / Lofi Beats (for outro's vibe)
- Future Garage (atmospheric, echoing vocal chops)

## Mood / vocal keywords
- Moods: Dreamy, Introspective, Melancholic, Relaxing, Atmospheric,
  Late Night, Emotional
- Instruments: Female Vocals, Ethereal Vocals, Soft Synth, Minimal Beat,
  Lush Pads

Note: Deep House (four-on-the-floor, faster) is a DIFFERENT vibe — this
brief matches slower, intimate material. Use Deep House tracks for action
edits; use this brief for reflective/scenic cuts.
